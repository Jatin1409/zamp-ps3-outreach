## Observed weaknesses (honest notes from the live runs)

- **Sensitive tagging is model-dependent.** In the thin-signal run, an item titled "Mesa ponders lawsuit against fire
  truck manufacturers" was not tagged sensitive by the classifier (it was not chosen as the hook, so no harm here).
- **Identity confidence is often "medium"** even for well-covered people, because the model tends to cite one
  source per candidate; the code only counts sources whose quotes it can verify.
- **Common names**: most public evidence for ordinary employees lives on LinkedIn, which is excluded by design, so
  the ambiguity check relies on press/company pages.
- **Drafts are plain and a little formulaic** ("Would you be open to a 2-line answer…"): safe and verifiable rather
  than clever. This is the free Flash-tier model; quality would be re-measured on a paid model.
- **Durations of 1–3 minutes** on the free tier (call spacing, retries, fallbacks, one regeneration for
  genericness in 2 of 6 drafts).
